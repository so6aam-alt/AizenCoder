AizenCoder Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

AizenCoder ("the app") is a Windows desktop program for recording, clipping and enhancing gameplay video. It is provided by [YOUR NAME OR STUDIO] ("we"). Contact: [YOUR EMAIL].

1. Your videos stay on your computer

Recording, clipping, enhancing and exporting all happen locally on your PC. We do not upload your videos to our servers, and we do not operate a video hosting service.

2. TikTok connection (optional)

If you choose "Connect TikTok account", the app uses TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API. You sign in on TikTok's own page; we never see your TikTok password. With your permission the app receives an access token, your TikTok open ID, display name and username. These are stored only on your computer, encrypted with Windows data protection (DPAPI), and are used only to upload or publish the videos you pick. When you upload, the video file is sent directly from your computer to TikTok. You can disconnect at any time in the app, which deletes the stored tokens, and you can revoke access in your TikTok settings.

3. Optional sign-in service

TikTok requires a secret key to complete sign-in. If the app is configured to use our small sign-in relay, it receives only the temporary authorization code from TikTok and passes it to TikTok to obtain your token. The relay does not store tokens, videos or personal data and keeps no logs of them.

4. Local data

The app keeps settings, local user profiles, export history and temporary working files on your computer (in your user profile folder). We do not receive this data. You can delete it by uninstalling the app and removing its folder.

5. What we do not do

We do not sell your data, do not show ads, do not use your data for advertising or profiling, and do not share your TikTok information with anyone other than TikTok.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).
